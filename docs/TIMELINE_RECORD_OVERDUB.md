# Timeline Record, Overdub and editing hardening — 0.2.7
Record and Overdub start at the Timeline playhead and switch to the existing Live surface. Media continues through that transition. Pause/resume/Stop+save are available in the global capture strip. Stop writes a new editable take clip directly into the original Timeline; the saved Recording copy is also retained. Cancel leaves the existing clips intact.

Record captures the performed resolved frame and holds existing automation while performing. Overdub continues rendering the original arrangement and captures only channels explicitly controlled by the performer, including effect channels. Its partial-channel take applies after the cue/FX lanes, preserving untouched automation. Manual channel ownership includes setting a value equal to its current value. Tempo is fixed during capture to preserve musical alignment. Media trims and offsets determine the correct source position; lead-in uses the internal clock until media starts.

FX lanes have persistent mute toggles and selection. Selected lanes receive clicked FX at the playhead and pasted cue clips. Muted lanes retain their editable clips and do not supply output or cue authority. Timeline Ctrl/Cmd A selects cue/video/take clips together; copy/paste preserves relative time and lane positions, with new identities and a single undo entry. Section, Cue, Stage and FX bank editing supports group clipboard actions; text fields retain native shortcuts.

UI hardening keeps transport and capture buttons readable with wrapping, consistent heights, controlled label widths and narrow-window spacing. External linking remains deferred. This is not the full source-neutral transport architecture; it reuses existing media and capture machinery for internal editing.
